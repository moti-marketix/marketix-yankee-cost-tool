Warehouse Costs

Real invoice total, normalized by carton size — items packed 1-per-box carry more of the handling cost per unit than items packed 10 or 20-per-box. Set the Market (and, for EU, Country) in the sidebar first — invoices you add or upload here are tagged to whichever one is currently selected. For EU, enter invoice amounts in EUR — they're automatically converted to USD using the live rate shown in the sidebar before being combined with Manufacturing/Freight costs.

Brand Exclusions

Check any brand that never touches this 3PL warehouse (e.g. ships direct to Amazon) — its items will show no Warehouse Cost at all, and won't count toward the shared Cost per Carton calculation for other brands.

Bulk Upload Invoices

Upload one or more invoice PDFs directly — the system reads the invoice date and total due automatically, matches or creates the right month row, and attaches the file. If a file can't be read automatically, it's flagged so you can enter that one manually.

Per-SKU Warehouse Cost

Total Warehouse Invoices ÷ Total "cartons handled" (units shipped to the 3PL warehouse ÷ units per carton, per SKU) = Cost per Carton. Each SKU's rate = Cost per Carton ÷ that SKU's own units per carton.

Upload Amazon Report(s)

Upload the SKU Economics report exactly as exported from Seller Central (.csv or .xlsx) — no template needed. Select multiple files at once (e.g. one per month) to import them all in a single action. Rows are matched to your Catalog by ASIN, and rows with zero activity that month are skipped automatically.

Upload Advertising Report (overwrites Ad Spend)

Upload the DSP-style advertising report (all campaign types, all markets, all months in one file) — this typically captures more ad types (headline, video) than Amazon's SKU Economics report. Spend is summed by ASIN + Month + Market and overwrites Ad Spend for exactly those combinations only; anything not in this file is left untouched. TACOS % recalculates automatically.
